switches

gv_06 · abc · xyz · fig.04

xyz left
[ frz ] grains freeze while hit is held or the gate is high
xyz mid
[ sync ] grain timing follows a clock or tap tempo
xyz right
[ trig ] grains play only on a trigger or a press
